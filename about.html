Background

Seema C.

I am Seema C., a contemporary figurative painter based in Bengaluru, India. My work draws from the everyday — the light on water, the movement of people on ghats and streets, the quiet intimacy of children at play. Painting, for me, is a way of paying attention to a world we often move through too quickly.

"Art is not what you see, but what you make others see."

I trained at [Your institution / self-taught background here] and have been exhibiting across India for over [X] years. My solo shows have been featured in [city / venue] and covered by publications including Vijayavani, Hosa Digantha, and Bharatha Sarathi.

My primary medium is acrylic on canvas. I am drawn to bold, fragmented backgrounds that contrast with detailed figurative subjects — a visual language that reflects the layered complexity of contemporary Indian life.

Teaching

Teaching is as central to my practice as painting itself. Over the years I have guided students of all ages and skill levels — from absolute beginners picking up a brush for the first time, to more experienced painters looking to deepen their understanding of composition and colour.

My classes are held in [location / online]. I believe every person has a visual story to tell; my role as a teacher is to help them find the language to tell it.
