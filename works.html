Lagoon Memoirs

Acrylic on Canvas  ·  48″ × 33″

Two boys at the edge of a lotus lagoon, engrossed in the quiet work of childhood. A fragmented, colour-block background gives way to tender figurative detail — the tension between abstraction and representation that defines much of my recent work.

Leap n Bounce

Acrylic on Canvas

Figures descend broad stone steps in a cascade of colour and movement. The painting captures a fleeting moment of everyday life — a mother, her children, the city stairs that connect them all.

Reminiscence
